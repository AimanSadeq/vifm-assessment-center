# VIFM High-Potential (HiPo) Model

**Scientific model brief - the framework behind the VIFM High-Potential Profile**

Version 1.0 · July 2026 · Original VIFM construction

---

## 1. What the model measures

High potential is the likelihood that an individual can rise into a more senior
role AND succeed once there. The VIFM model reads it through two measured
pillars plus one manager-rated element, all on a shared 1-5 scale:

| Pillar | Question | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Aspiration** | Will they rise? | VIFM Persona® - eight drive/growth markers |
| **Ability** | Can they succeed at the next level? | VIFM Persona® behavioural competencies + VIFM Logica® reasoning |
| **Engagement** | Will they stay? | Six-item survey rated by the line manager |

The two measured pillars place the individual on a **nine-grid** (3 x 3) of
Aspiration x Ability. Engagement never moves the grid placement - it annotates
what to DO with the placement, because commitment to stay cannot be read from
an assessment sitting and a manager judgement should shape the conversation,
not the arithmetic.

## 2. Pillar 1 - Aspiration (the eight markers)

Aspiration is the mean of eight behavioural competencies from the VIFM
41-competency framework, selected because they evidence drive, initiative,
growth appetite, and the courage to step up:

1. Proactive Initiative
2. Outcome Ownership
3. Accountability for Commitments
4. Learning by Doing
5. Mobilising Around Purpose
6. Principled Courage
7. Adaptive Learning Capacity
8. Continuous Self-Development

These are self-rated in the Persona sitting (forced-choice and Likert items,
reverse-keyed). Self-report shows how the individual sees their own drive; the
report directs the reader to triangulate with observed behaviour and, where
available, a Reflect 360® view.

## 3. Pillar 2 - Ability (a stated 60/40 blend)

Ability blends two evidence streams with **stated weights** so the blend is
never a black box:

- **Behavioural ability (60%)** - the mean of the remaining measured Persona
  competencies (everything outside the aspiration set): the leadership-relevant
  behavioural repertoire.
- **Cognitive agility (40%)** - the VIFM Logica® reasoning composite, mapped
  from accuracy onto the shared 1-5 scale.

**Recommended Logica configuration:** Inductive reasoning (the closest proxy
for learning agility - spotting patterns in unfamiliar material predicts how
quickly someone grows into a bigger, less-defined role) and Numerical
reasoning (senior roles in the region's banking, government and corporate
context are data- and financially-loaded) are required; Deductive reasoning is
recommended for senior or governance-heavy pipelines. When no Logica result is
attached, Ability rests on behavioural evidence alone and the report says so.

## 4. The nine-grid

Each axis is banded **Developing** (below 3.0), **Solid** (3.0-3.8) and
**Strong** (3.8+). The nine cells each carry a VIFM archetype with a narrative
and a development focus:

| | Ability: Developing | Ability: Solid | Ability: Strong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Aspiration: Strong** | Eager Developer - Build Foundations | Emerging Potential - Stretch with Support | High Potential - Accelerate |
| **Aspiration: Solid** | Steady Performer - Focus Development | Solid Contributor - Grow Steadily | Quiet Strength - Ignite Ambition |
| **Aspiration: Developing** | Early Journey - Foundational Development | Developing Contributor | Untapped Expert - Re-engage |

The report renders the grid as a colour-graded chart (blue tonal zones
deepening toward the high-potential corner) with drawn X/Y axes and the
individual plotted in their cell.

## 5. Engagement (manager-rated)

Around twenty observable statements answered by the line manager on a 1-5
scale, taking about five minutes: stay-intent and retention-risk signals
(reverse-keyed), discretionary effort, acting on development, purpose and
values alignment, appetite for bigger responsibility, advocacy, pride, energy,
ownership, resilience, relationships, long-term orientation, learning,
initiative, reliability, and whether they would recommend the organisation. The
score renders as a third
gauge, and specific combinations produce an overlay note - for example, a
High Potential placement with Developing engagement is flagged "secure
engagement before heavy development investment."

This is a single-rater management judgement, prone to recency and halo
effects. The report labels it as such and treats it as a conversation opener,
never a verdict.

## 6. Development output

The report closes with a development plan built from the three lowest-rated
behavioural competencies (with actions drawn from the VIFM development-tip
bank) plus reasoning-practice activities for any Logica subtest below the
Solid band.

## 7. Honest limits

- Band cuts are scale-midpoint based and **indicative until a VIFM norm
  sample is established**.
- Aspiration and behavioural Ability are self-reported; cognitive Ability is
  performance-tested; Engagement is a single-rater judgement. The report
  states each source next to its number.
- The profile is **one input to a high-potential nomination, never the
  decision itself**. No score in this model is a pass/fail signal.
- A sitting must cover at least four aspiration markers AND at least four
  broader competencies, or the report refuses to generate rather than
  fabricate a pillar.

## 8. Delivery

Generated from a Bespoke Bundle sitting (Persona + Logica completed in one
session). Available to VIFM staff and to the client organisation's portal;
client-downloaded copies show "manager-rated" without naming the individual
manager, and the manager's free-text context is consultant-visible only.
